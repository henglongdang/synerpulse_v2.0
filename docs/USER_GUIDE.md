# FamilyPulse User Guide

FamilyPulse keeps your household's money in one shared place: the accounts you share, what's been spent, the bills coming up, what you're saving for, and a planner that shows where things are heading. Everyone you invite sees the same numbers, and each person can do as much (or as little) as their role allows.

## 1. Getting started
1. **Create an account** with your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters.
2. **Name your household** and choose its currency.
3. **Follow the short setup** (about three minutes). You can stop at any time with *Finish later*; the dashboard will offer to pick up where you left off.
   - **Accounts:** add the accounts you share (checking, savings, cards, loans, investments) with today's balance. For a card or loan, enter what you owe.
   - **Income:** add each paycheck or income stream and how often it arrives. If it varies (freelance, tips), tick *This income varies* and enter what you'd get in a slow month.
   - **Spend:** tap the categories you want to watch, then give each a monthly limit.
   - **Goals:** add what you're saving for, such as a down payment or a college fund.
   - **Confirm:** review the plan and confirm.

Joining someone else's household? Open the invitation link they sent you, then create an account or sign in.

## 2. Moving around
- **Computer:** the menu on the left, plus *Add entry*, search, notifications and your account menu at the top.
- **Phone:** tabs along the bottom (Spend, Budget, Dashboard, Planner) and the **+ Add** button. Tap your picture (top left) for Settings, Household and Sign out.
- **Household / Personal** (top of Dashboard and Spend): *Household* shows shared accounts; *Personal* shows only your private accounts.

## 3. Dashboard
- **Net worth:** everything the accounts in view hold, minus what's owed, with a chart for the last 1M, 6M, 1Y or All.
- **Last 7 days:** income and spending compared with the week before, plus savings and loans.
- **This month's budget:** how much of the month's limit is spent. The tick on the bar marks today, so you can see whether you're ahead of or behind pace.
- **Next 3 months:** the lowest your shared cash is expected to get. If it might run short, you'll see a red warning.
- **Smart tips:** reminders worked out from your own data, such as being close to a limit, spending more than usual, or a charge that repeats every month. Tap *How we worked this out* to see the reasoning. Tips follow simple, transparent rules; they are not financial advice.
- **Upcoming bills:** tap **Paid** when you've paid one; it's added to the ledger automatically.

## 4. Spend (the ledger)
- **Add an entry:** choose Spending, Income or Transfer, then enter the amount, date, description, account and category. Tags (for example `school, freelance`) help with searching and automation.
- **Find entries:** search by description, merchant or notes; filter by account, category, type or dates; sort by date or amount; export what you see as CSV.
- **Open an entry** to see who logged it, its notes and receipts, and three tabs:
  - **Comments:** ask questions or add context. Tap a name (for example *@Mary*) to mention someone; they'll get a notification.
  - **History:** every change, who made it, and when. Tap *Restore* to go back to an earlier version.
  - **Receipts:** attach a photo or PDF (up to 5 MB). On a phone this can open the camera.
- **Edit or delete:** deleted entries can be undone immediately and stay in the history. If someone else changed an entry while you were editing it, FamilyPulse shows both versions and lets you choose.
- **Breakdown:** what was spent in a month, by group (Living, Entertainment, Unexpected) and category. Tap a category to see its entries.
- **Import a bank statement:** *Import CSV* → choose the account and file → match the columns → *Preview* → *Import*. Entries already in the account are detected and skipped.
- **Offline?** You can still add entries. They show under *Saved on this device* and are sent automatically when you're back online.

## 5. Budget
- Use the month arrows to look back or ahead.
- Each group shows spending against its limit; open a group to see its categories. *On track*, *Near limit* and *Over* are shown with an icon and a word, not just colour. Categories paid by a scheduled bill (rent, for example) are marked **Bill**, and reaching 100% there is expected.
- **Set budget** changes the limits from that month onward. *Reset this month* brings back the previous plan.
- **Saving goals:** see progress, the monthly amount needed to hit your date, and whether you're on track. *Add money* records a contribution or withdrawal.
- **Recurring:** your bills and income schedule. *Mark paid* (or *Received*) adds the entry and moves the schedule forward; *Skip* moves it forward without an entry.

## 6. Planner: "what if…?"
1. Pick a horizon (3 months to 2 years), monthly or weekly detail, and *Expected* or *Conservative* income.
2. Choose a starting point, such as *Income drops 30%*, *Delay a payment 2 weeks*, *A big one-off expense*, *Spend 10% less day-to-day*, or *A new monthly cost*. Adjust it with the sliders, or combine several.
3. Compare **As things are** with **With your changes**. The planner tells you the day cash would first run out, if it would, and the lowest point.
4. *How this is calculated* lists exactly what it assumed.
5. **Save scenario** to share it with your household.

## 7. Household (owners and admins)

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the household and transferring ownership |
| Admin | Manage members, accounts, budgets, goals, bills and rules; edit any shared entry |
| Contributor | Add and edit shared entries (optionally only in chosen categories), mark bills paid, add to goals, save scenarios, comment |
| Viewer | See shared information and comment |

- **Invite:** choose a role (and, for contributors, the categories they may edit), optionally restrict the link to an email address, and share the link. It works once and expires in 7 days.
- **Accounts:** add, archive or delete accounts. *Update balance* records statement balances (for example for investments) as a "balance update" entry, so history stays complete.
- **Private accounts** are visible only to you. Nobody else sees them, not even owners or admins, and they don't count toward household totals.
- **Categories:** add, rename or archive.
- **Automation:** rules that run when entries are added. For example, *When income is tagged "freelance" → put 20% toward "Down payment"*.

## 8. Notifications and activity
- The **bell** collects budget warnings, bill reminders, cash-shortfall warnings, goal milestones, mentions and changes to your access. Choose which you get in *Settings → Alerts & nudges*.
- **Activity** is the household's history: who added, changed or commented on what.

## 9. Settings, privacy and your data
- Change your name or password, see the devices you're signed in on, and sign them out.
- **Data sources** shows where numbers come from. FamilyPulse never asks for your online-banking password, and automatic bank syncing isn't connected.
- **Download my data** gives you everything you can see, as a file.
- **Delete account** permanently removes your login and private accounts. If you own a household with other members, transfer ownership first.

## 10. Install on your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** menu → *Install app*.

It opens like an app. Your financial data is always loaded fresh and is never stored for offline viewing.
